A simpler way to work in NetSuite.

Before you start To avoid conflicts and get the most out of NSFT, we recommend disabling any other NetSuite extension you have installed. See the three quick steps below.

Heads up: NSFT is now compatible with Redwood, NetSuite's new interface. Occasional issues may still appear — if you spot one, please help us by reporting it through the "Report a bug" button so we can fix it as soon as possible.

We know how it feels

Working in NetSuite usually means stacking several extensions: one to run SuiteQL, another to inspect the record object, another to audit scripts, another to copy field IDs… and so on.

Over time, they pile up. They overlap, clash with each other, and end up affecting performance.

NSFT brings those tools together into a single extension — a more consistent, lightweight experience. The idea is simple: fewer pieces, better workflow.
